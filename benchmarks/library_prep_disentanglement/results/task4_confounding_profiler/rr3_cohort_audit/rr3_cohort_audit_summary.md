# RR3 cohort audit summary

- Samples: 39d 2 FLT/2 GC; 40d 3 FLT/2 GC; 41d 1 FLT/2 GC.
- All cohorts share BALB/c female mouse liver, age, ribodepletion, paired 150-bp sequencing, UC Davis, liquid-nitrogen preservation, diet, habitat, euthanasia method, and stranded library processing.
- Animal identity, duration/exposure, collection cohort, radiation dose for FLT animals, RIN, read depth, rRNA contamination, and library index differ. Exact euthanasia times/order and dissection times/order are unavailable locally.
- Duration and collection/animal cohort are inseparable; no independent duration replication exists.
- Leave-one-out directional ranges: {39: {'min': -0.7233469620987973, 'max': -0.6721378575898991, 'median': -0.6959761345313797}, 40: {'min': 0.5687291641775412, 'max': 0.8697095722201732, 'median': 0.7472355073231358}, 41: {'min': 0.19267101738868803, 'max': 0.6753825878841707, 'median': 0.43402680263642934}}.
- Bridge response-vector cosine between 39d and 40d: -0.5674. The conventional expression response cosine is recorded separately in rr3_flt_gc_decomposition.csv.
- Classification: **G. MIXED / UNRESOLVED**. The configurations are technically reproducible but not biologically explained as a duration transition.
