Each row is an individual study; columns are GO Biological Process 2025, KEGG Human 2021 and Reactome Pathways 2024. BRIDGE and DE each contribute 500 genes selected independently from the same study-specific eligible universe. No gene intersection or cross-study pathway recurrence filter is applied. Displayed terms are the union of the three lowest-q passing terms for each method within each study/library, ordered by the smaller q. For each displayed term, blue and orange bars show passing BRIDGE and DE results respectively; a missing bar means that method did not meet the reporting criteria, not zero enrichment. Terms may occur for both methods. All significant terms and exact overlap counts are available in significant_pathways.csv. One-sided hypergeometric tests use study-specific eligible, library-annotated backgrounds; BH correction is joint across all three libraries per query. Reporting requires q<0.05, overlap>=3, and background term size 10–500. Bar length measures statistical evidence, not fold enrichment; annotations overlap, and a difference in significance is not a direct statistical test between methods.
