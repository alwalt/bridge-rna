Cross-study reproducibility from matched top-500 gene lists. (a) Number of genes selected in all three studies for each condition. BRIDGE and DE each select 500 genes independently per study from the same eligible universe. (b) Number of terms enriched independently in all three study-specific top-500 lists, separated by annotation library. Each enrichment test uses the study-specific eligible, library-annotated background, one-sided hypergeometric tests, and BH correction jointly across GO Biological Process 2025, KEGG Human 2021, and Reactome Pathways 2024 per query. Passing terms require q<0.05, at least three overlapping genes, and background term size 10–500. Panel b does not enrich the gene intersections in panel a; contributing genes may differ across studies. Annotation terms overlap and counts are not independent biological discoveries. Zero labels indicate no passing terms. Counts are descriptive, without uncertainty intervals or a direct statistical test of method differences. DE has more significant study–term results overall (605 versus 272 for BRIDGE); this figure addresses recurrence rather than total within-study enrichment.
